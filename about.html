Zammane Business Enterprise

Founded in 2001, ZAMMANE is a leading industry player specializing in diverse products and services. Our expertise in design, contract documentation, and construction supervision allows us to develop tailored risk management strategies for each project.
With a team of highly qualified engineers and geologists, we prioritize delivering technically precise solutions that meet our clients’ unique needs. As a 100% Black-Owned B-BBEE LEVEL 1 entity, we foster close, collaborative partnerships with all stakeholders.
What sets us apart is our commitment to customized solutions and a problem-solving approach. We design, manufacture, and integrate connected infrastructure to enhance safety and operational efficiency. Our respectful demeanor, rooted in African culture, positions ZAMMANE as a trusted partner for your diverse requirements.

  • Expertise in Gas Insulated Switchgear and Relay Testing
  • Custom solutions for power plants, substations, and process industries
  • Energetic team delivering top-tier service and maintenance
